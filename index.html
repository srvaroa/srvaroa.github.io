Projects

  • GPU infrastructure for AI workloads Midokura, now

    Systems to provision, manage and operate GPU infrastructure and high-bandwidth fabric in private datacenters. Multi-tenant IaaS and PaaS layers for AI workload orchestration.

  • Sony Edge AI Sensing Platform Midokura, about 3 years

    Re-architected the real-time inference streaming backend: 100x performance with a lower resource footprint. Moved release from manual QA to automated pipelines: cycle time cut by more than 50%, across EU and Japan teams.

  • Entity Platform at New Relic 2020 to 2022

    The inventory of everything New Relic observes: hosts, containers, services, their metadata and relationships. Most of the observability product depended on it. It was on the critical path of 50 to 75% of company OKRs, under a 99.8% SLA. Led the design of the telemetry analysis system that processed millions of datapoints per second and inferred entity types and relationships from open rules.

  • Internal developer platform for 1,500 engineers at Adevinta 2016 to 2020

    Kubernetes PaaS for development, delivery and operations, used by marketplaces across the group in Europe and South America. Led the migration of existing workloads from on-premise and EC2 onto it. Developer tooling from zero to hundreds of users.

  • Network virtualization for OpenStack Midokura, 2013 to 2016

    SDN control and data plane: L2 and VxLAN gateways, NAT, hardware VTEP integration.

  • Music catalogue ingestion at Last.fm 2007 to 2009

    Managed the systems that ingested and served the largest streaming music catalogue in the world at the time, with 60M tracks, 40M users.
